## Description

Remember what you read with Dory.

Dory turns your PDFs, Word documents, Markdown, pasted notes, and other study
material into focused flashcards. Generated cards retain source context, so you
can check where an answer came from before committing it to memory.

STUDY AT THE RIGHT TIME

Review with FSRS, a modern spaced-repetition scheduler that adapts to your grades.
Dory keeps a clear due queue and gives every card familiar Again, Hard, Good, and
Easy choices.

BRING THE DECKS YOU ALREADY HAVE

Import Anki .apkg decks or text-based card lists. Your existing material belongs
in the same library as the cards you create with Dory.

KEEP REVIEWING OFFLINE

Recent decks remain available when you lose your connection. Study grades queue
on your device and sync automatically after you reconnect.

FREE FOR STUDY, PRO FOR GENERATION

Unlimited studying, FSRS scheduling, imports, and offline review are free. Dory
Pro unlocks AI card generation for $3.99 per month or $29.99 per year.
